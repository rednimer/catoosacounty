Storage Size Tips

📏

Size Up If Unsure

It's better to have a little extra room than to cram everything in. A slightly larger unit costs a few dollars more but saves time and frustration.

📦

Stack Smart

Use uniform boxes, stack heavy on bottom, and leave an aisle down the center so you can access items without unpacking everything.

❄

Consider Climate Control

If you're storing wood furniture, electronics, documents, or photos, climate-controlled storage protects against North Georgia heat and humidity.
