Directions

From Fort Oglethorpe

Head south on Battlefield Parkway toward Cloud Springs Road. Turn onto Cloud Springs Road and continue to Mack Smith Road. Turn right. Facility is on your right. About 5 minutes.

From Downtown Rossville

Take McFarland Avenue north toward Cloud Springs Road. Turn left onto Cloud Springs Road, then right onto Mack Smith Road. Less than 3 minutes.

From East Ridge / Chattanooga

Take Ringgold Road south into Rossville. Turn right onto Cloud Springs Road, then right onto Mack Smith Road. Just past the state line.

From I-75 (Exit 350)

Exit at Battlefield Parkway. Head west through Fort Oglethorpe, follow Cloud Springs Road to Mack Smith Road. Quick interstate access.
