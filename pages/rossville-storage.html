How to Find Us in Rossville

We're easy to reach from anywhere in the Rossville, Fort Oglethorpe, and Chattanooga area.

From Downtown Rossville

Head north on McFarland Avenue toward Cloud Springs Road. Turn left onto Cloud Springs Road, then turn right onto Mack Smith Road. The facility is on your right, less than 3 minutes from the center of town.

From Fort Oglethorpe

Take Battlefield Parkway south toward Cloud Springs Road. Turn onto Cloud Springs Road and continue to Mack Smith Road. Turn right. We're about 5 minutes from downtown Fort Oglethorpe.

From East Ridge, TN

Take Ringgold Road south across the state line into Rossville. Turn right onto Cloud Springs Road, then right onto Mack Smith Road. Just a few minutes past the Georgia-Tennessee border.

Near Cloud Springs Road

If you're already on Cloud Springs Road, look for the Mack Smith Road intersection. Turn onto Mack Smith Road and the facility entrance is immediately visible on the right side.
