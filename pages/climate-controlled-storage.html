Why Climate Control Matters in North Georgia

North Georgia summers bring temperatures above 90°F with humidity levels that can exceed 80%. Inside an unregulated storage unit, those conditions accelerate damage to wood, leather, paper, electronics, and fabric.

Our climate-controlled units maintain a stable interior environment year-round, keeping temperature and humidity within a safe range. That means no warping wood, no mildewed upholstery, no corroded electronics, and no faded photographs.

If you're storing anything you'd keep inside your home, climate-controlled storage is the right choice.

What Needs Climate-Controlled Storage?

If you'd keep it inside your house, it belongs in a climate-controlled unit.

🛒

Furniture

Wood warps and cracks. Leather dries out. Upholstery absorbs moisture and develops mildew. Climate control keeps furniture in the condition you stored it.

💻

Electronics

Condensation and humidity corrode circuit boards, screens, and internal components. Climate control prevents moisture damage to TVs, computers, and audio equipment.

📄

Documents & Photos

Paper yellows, warps, and grows mold in humid conditions. Keep tax records, legal documents, family photos, and books safe with stable climate storage.

🎨

Artwork & Collectibles

Paintings, prints, vinyl records, stamps, coins, and other collectibles deteriorate in extreme temperatures. Climate control preserves their condition and value.

🎸

Musical Instruments

Guitars warp. Piano keys stick. Brass tarnishes. Instruments are sensitive to temperature and humidity swings. Climate storage keeps them playable.

👚

Clothing & Fabrics

Moisture breeds mildew and odors in stored clothing, linens, quilts, and seasonal fabrics. Climate control keeps textiles fresh and clean.
