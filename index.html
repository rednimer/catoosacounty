Serving Rossville, Fort Oglethorpe & Catoosa County

Our location on Mack Smith Road puts you close to storage no matter where you are in the area.

From Fort Oglethorpe

Head south on Battlefield Parkway toward Cloud Springs Road. Turn onto Cloud Springs Road, then turn right onto Mack Smith Road. The facility is on your right. About 5 minutes from downtown Fort Oglethorpe.

From Downtown Rossville

Take McFarland Avenue north toward Cloud Springs Road. Turn left onto Cloud Springs Road, then right onto Mack Smith Road. Less than 3 minutes from the center of Rossville.

From East Ridge / Chattanooga

Take Ringgold Road south into Rossville. Turn right onto Cloud Springs Road, then right again onto Mack Smith Road. Just minutes past the state line from East Ridge, TN.

From I-75

Exit I-75 at Exit 350 (Battlefield Parkway). Head west through Fort Oglethorpe toward Cloud Springs Road. Follow Cloud Springs Road to Mack Smith Road. Easy access from the interstate.
